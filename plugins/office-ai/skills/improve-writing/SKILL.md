# Improve writing

1. Ask for the reader and the purpose if they are not clear.
2. Find the problems and list them first, quoting each: sentences over 25 words, long words where a short one works, stacked clauses, passive chains, filler openings ("It is important to note"), lists of three adjectives, repeated structure.
3. Show a short table: problem, quote, suggested fix.
4. Rewrite only the flagged parts. Keep every number, name, date, quote and claim unchanged.
5. Do not shorten by dropping content: a shorter text that loses a fact is worse.
6. Return the text plus the list of what changed; the user accepts or rejects.
7. Check: compare numbers and names before and after; list any difference.
