name: find-contradictions
description: Finds statements in a document that contradict each other, such as one quantity with two values or a term with two meanings, and reports each with its places and quotes. Use when a report, plan or contract must be checked for consistency.
---

# Find contradictions

1. Read the whole document. Collect at most 40 claims that carry a number, a definition, a name, a date or an order of events.
2. Write them as a table: id, subject, value, place (page, section or table), exact quote.
3. Group the claims by subject. A conflict needs two claims about the same subject with different values.
4. Open each candidate conflict in the document and confirm it. A rounding difference or a different period is not a conflict: say so.
5. Report each confirmed conflict with both places and both quotes. Never report one you did not confirm.
6. An empty result is a valid answer: say "no conflicts found among N claims".
7. Quote the document; do not paraphrase, so the reader can find the sentence.
