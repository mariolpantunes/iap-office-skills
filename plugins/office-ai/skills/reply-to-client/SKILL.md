# Reply to a client

1. Read the e-mail; list the problem, the demand and the deadline, quoting the client.
2. Draft the reply in the client's language (European Portuguese for Portuguese mails): thank, acknowledge the problem without admitting legal blame, propose one concrete next step.
3. Make no commitments the user did not give you: no refund, compensation, delivery date, **response time** ("within two days") or follow-up date. Promise only that the e-mail was received and will be handled.
4. Name no person or contact that the e-mail or the user did not give: write MISSING and let the user fill it.
5. End with a checklist: order number, item, quantity and dates, each compared with the e-mail; then list every promise or time limit that appears in the draft (none is the best answer).
6. Deliver a draft only; a person reads it and presses send.
