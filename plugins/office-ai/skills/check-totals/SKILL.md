description: Checks row counts and totals of a cleaned table against its sources before any chart or report. Use after consolidating or analysing data, or when a number must be verified.
---

# Check totals

1. Count the data lines of every source file and the rows of the clean table; they must differ only by the duplicates you removed. Show the arithmetic.
2. Recompute the grand total of the amount column from the clean table and compare it with the figure in the report or chart.
3. Pick one group (one client and one month), list its rows and add them by hand.
4. If any of the three checks fails, stop. Say which one and show the rows behind the number.
5. Only when all three pass, state: "rows OK, total OK, one group OK".
